## Example Structure for Location Generation Prompt:

```prompt
**[Location_Type]** in [Time_of_Day/Weather_Description], [Overall_Mood_or_Atmosphere], **[Key_Architectural_or_Environmental_Features]**, [Specific_Details_like_Flora_Fauna_Objects], [Lighting_Description], **epic wide shot**, **cinematic view**, intricate details, fantasy art, photorealistic --ar 16:9 [--no [Negative_Elements]] [--c [Chaos_Value]] [--p [Personalization_Style]]
```

### Explanation of Fields:

*   **[Location_Type]**: e.g., Ancient ruins, bustling marketplace, serene forest, futuristic city, hidden cave.
*   **[Time_of_Day/Weather_Description]**: e.g., at sunset, during a stormy night, under a clear morning sky, with a light drizzle.
*   **[Overall_Mood_or_Atmosphere]**: e.g., mystical, eerie, vibrant, desolate, peaceful.
*   **[Key_Architectural_or_Environmental_Features]**: Prominent structures, natural formations, or landmarks.
*   **[Specific_Details_like_Flora_Fauna_Objects]**: Smaller elements that add detail and life to the scene.
*   **[Lighting_Description]**: How the scene is illuminated, e.g., warm glow, harsh shadows, soft moonlight.
*   **"epic wide shot"**: Specifies the camera angle and scope.
*   **"cinematic view"**: Adds a film-like quality to the image.
*   **"intricate details, fantasy art, photorealistic"**: Artistic style and level of realism.
*   **"--ar 16:9"**: Sets the aspect ratio of the generated image.
*   **"[--no [Negative_Elements]]"**: Optional. Use to exclude unwanted elements (e.g., `--no blurry, distorted`).
*   **"[--c [Chaos_Value]]"**: Optional. Controls the randomness/variety (0-100).
*   **"[--p [Personalization_Style]]"**: Optional. A specific style or artist to emulate.
