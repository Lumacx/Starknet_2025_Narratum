# User Guide: Getting Started with Narratum

Welcome to Narratum, your portal to interactive storytelling! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to discover, create, and manage your own unique narratives.

## Discovering Stories

The Narratum homepage and Discover page are your gateways to a world of stories.

### Browsing and Filtering

You can browse stories by various categories and genres. Use the filtering options to narrow down your choices and find exactly what you're looking for, including filtering by story type, plan type, language, and author.

### Semantic Search

Narratum features an intelligent semantic search. Instead of just matching keywords, you can describe the *kind* of story you're looking for, and Narratum will surface stories that semantically match your intent, even if they don't contain the exact words.

### Reading, Rating, and Favoriting

Once you find a story that piques your interest, you can:
*   **Read:** Dive into the narrative with our immersive Story Reader. Note that some stories may require credits to read, depending on their plan type.
*   **Rate:** Share your thoughts and help others discover great content by rating stories using our star rating system. You earn rating stars by reading stories.
*   **Favorite:** Add stories to your favorites for quick access later from your Profile page.
*   **Tip Writers:** Show your appreciation to creators by sending them credits as a tip directly from the story card.

## Account Management

While you can browse and read some stories anonymously, logging in unlocks the full suite of Narratum features, including story creation, saving drafts, and managing your profile.

### Sign Up / Log In Options

Narratum offers flexible authentication methods:
*   **Email/Password:** Create a traditional account with your email.
*   **Google Sign-In:** Use your existing Google account for a quick and easy login.
*   **Starknet Wallet:** For our blockchain-savvy users, connect your Starknet wallet to access Narratum. Your Starknet address will be linked to an anonymous Firebase user, allowing you to seamlessly use all features while maintaining your web3 identity.

### Credits

Narratum utilizes a credit system for accessing premium stories and features. Your current credit balance is displayed and managed within your user profile. Credits can be purchased or earned through various in-app activities.

## Creating Your First Story

The `/create/begin` flow is where your storytelling journey truly begins.

### Starting a New Story vs. Continuing a Draft

*   **Begin a New Tale:** Start fresh with a brand new story idea.
*   **Continue a Draft:** Pick up where you left off on an unfinished story. Narratum automatically saves your progress locally, so your ideas are always safe.

### Filling in Story Details

Provide the foundational information for your story:
*   **Title:** A captivating name for your narrative.
*   **Genres:** Select relevant genres to help other users discover your story.
*   **Synopsis:** A brief overview of your story's plot and themes.
*   **Category:** The main category your story belongs to.
*   **Pages:** The estimated number of pages for your story.
*   **Language:** The language your story is written in.
*   **Campaign Name (Optional):** If your story is part of a larger campaign, specify its name.

### Managing Your Story's Cover Image

The `CoverImageManager` component helps you set the perfect visual for your story:
*   **Upload:** Upload your own cover image.
*   **AI Description:** Narratum can analyze your uploaded image and provide an AI-generated description, which can be helpful for accessibility and further AI-driven features.

### Understanding and Configuring Premium Features

Narratum offers exciting premium features to enhance your stories:
*   **Convai Agent ID:** Integrate a Convai AI agent for dynamic character interactions within your story. Enter the agent ID here.
*   **Teaser Video URL:** Add a teaser video to intrigue your readers. Provide the video's URL.
*   **Free Navigation Index:** Define how far into your story users can navigate freely before hitting a paywall or premium content boundary.

### Saving and Progressing Through Creation

Your story progress is continuously saved as a local draft. You can navigate between creation steps, and Narratum will ensure your work is preserved.

## Building Story References & AI Support

The `/create/support` page is your creative hub, offering powerful AI tools and asset management.

### Overview of Tabs

The `AI References & Support` section is organized into tabs:
*   **Characters:** Manage your story's characters.
*   **Locations:** Define the settings for your narrative.
*   **Audio Narrations:** Store and organize audio for character dialogue or descriptive narration.
*   **Audio Effects:** Manage sound effects to enrich your story's atmosphere.
*   **Videos:** Integrate video clips into your story.

### How to Upload and Manage Assets in Your Gallery

Within each tab, you can:
*   **Upload:** Easily upload your own images, audio, or video files.
*   **Manage Gallery:** Organize and view all your uploaded assets.

### Using AI to Generate New Images

*   **Single Asset Generation:** Provide a text prompt, and Narratum's AI will generate a unique image for your characters, locations, or other visual needs.

### Using AI to Compose New Scenes

*   **Scene Composer:** Combine multiple existing assets from your gallery with a text prompt to create a complex and dynamic new scene image. The AI intelligently blends the visual elements with your textual description.

### Leveraging AI to Describe Existing Images

*   **AI Image Description:** Upload an image, and Narratum's AI can generate a detailed textual description of its content. This is useful for improving image searchability and accessibility.

### Accessing "Generation Tips" for Better Prompting

Look for the `InfoPopover` elements throughout the creation process. These provide valuable "Generation Tips" to help you craft more effective prompts for AI image generation and other AI features.

## Your Dashboard

The Dashboard (`/dashboard`) provides insights into your storytelling journey.

### Understanding Story Statistics and Comparisons

*   **All App vs. My Stories:** Compare the performance of your stories against the overall Narratum community.
*   **By Genre, Type, Plan:** Analyze story engagement based on genre, story type, and premium plan status.

## Your Profile

The Profile page (`/profile`) is your personal hub on Narratum.

### Managing Your Avatar

*   **Avatar Upload:** Personalize your profile by uploading a unique avatar.

### Viewing Your Stories

*   **Published Stories:** See all the stories you've published to the Narratum community.
*   **Drafts:** Access your unfinished stories that are saved as drafts.
*   **Favorites:** Revisit the stories you've marked as favorites from other creators.

### Real-time Updates for User-Specific Data

Your profile page provides real-time updates for your story statistics, favorite list, and drafts, ensuring you always have the most current information.

## Reading Stories

The Narratum Story Reader is designed for an immersive and personalized reading experience.

### Story Navigation

*   **Intuitive Controls:** Easily navigate through story pages with clear forward and backward controls.
*   **Chapter/Section Navigation:** (If applicable to story structure) Quickly jump to different parts of the story.

### Audio Controls

*   **Background Music:** Toggle and control ambient background music to set the mood.
*   **Narration Playback:** Play, pause, and control the volume of audio narrations for character dialogue or descriptive passages.
*   **Page-Turn SFX:** Enable or disable subtle sound effects when turning pages for a more tactile reading experience.

### Font Scaling

*   **Personalized Reading:** Adjust the font size to your preference for optimal readability, ensuring comfort for all users.

### Dynamic Background Images

*   **Immersive Visuals:** Stories can feature dynamic background images that change with the narrative, enhancing the visual storytelling experience.

## Conclusion

This guide should provide a solid foundation for your Narratum journey. We encourage you to explore the platform and unleash your creativity!
